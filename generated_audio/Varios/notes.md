## 2. Formal B2/C1 Example Sentences

Here are two formal sentences for each key term, suitable for academic or professional contexts.

### **To vary**
1.  *"The curriculum is designed to **vary** according to the specific needs and interests of the students."*

2.  *"It is crucial that we **vary** our communication strategies to ensure maximum engagement with diverse audiences."*
    *   *(Es crucial que **varíemos** nuestras estrategias de comunicación para asegurar la máxima participación con audiencias diversas.)*

### **To adapt**
1.  *"The company must **adapt** its business model to remain competitive in a rapidly changing global market."*
    *   *(La empresa debe **adaptar** su modelo de negocio para mantenerse competitiva en un mercado global cambiante rápidamente.)*
2.  *"Successful learners are those who can quickly **adapt** their study habits to different educational environments."*
    *   *(Los estudiantes exitosos son aquellos que pueden **adaptar** rápidamente sus hábitos de estudio a diferentes entornos educativos.)*

### **To adjust**
1.  *"We need to **adjust** our expectations to align with the current economic realities of the region."*
    *   *(Necesitamos **ajustar** nuestras expectativas para alinearse con las realidades económicas actuales de la región.)*
2.  *"The software allows users to **adjust** the sensitivity settings to suit their personal preferences."*
    *   *(El software permite a los usuarios **ajustar** los ajustes de sensibilidad para adaptarse a sus preferencias personales.)*

### **To fluctuate**
1.  *"Exchange rates tend to **fluctuate** significantly during periods of high geopolitical uncertainty."*
    *   *(Las tasas de cambio tienden a **fluctuar** significativamente durante períodos de alta incertidumbre geopolítica.)*
2.  *"Temperature levels in the greenhouse **fluctuate** depending on the time of day and external weather conditions."*
    *   *(Los niveles de temperatura en la invernadero **fluctúan** dependiendo de la hora del día y las condiciones climáticas externas.)*

### **To diversify**
1.  *"Investors often seek to **diversify** their portfolios by including assets from different sectors."*
    *   *(Los inversores a menudo buscan **diversificar** sus carteras incluyendo activos de diferentes sectores.)*
2.  *"The team aims to **diversify** its recruitment sources to attract talent from underrepresented backgrounds."*
    *   *(El equipo busca **diversificar** sus fuentes de reclutamiento para atraer talento de orígenes subrepresentados.)*

### **To accommodate**
1.  *"The hotel management promised to **accommodate** all dietary requirements of the guests without exception."*
    *   *(La gerencia del hotel prometió **acomodar** todos los requisitos dietéticos de los huéspedes sin excepción.)*
2.  *"We must be flexible enough to **accommodate** last-minute changes in the project schedule."*
    *   *(Debemos ser lo suficientemente flexibles para **acomodar** cambios de última hora en el cronograma del proyecto.)*

### **To distinguish**
1.  *"It is important to **distinguish** between correlation and causation when analyzing statistical data."*
    *   *(Es importante **distinguir** entre correlación y causalidad al analizar datos estadísticos.)*
2.  *"The new policy aims to **distinguish** between minor infractions and serious violations of the code."*
    *   *(La nueva política busca **distinguir** entre infracciones menores y violaciones graves del código.)*

1.  *"Could you please **elaborate** on your previous point regarding the impact of technology on education?"*

1.  *"Digital tools are essential to **facilitate** remote collaboration among distributed teams."*

2.  *"The mediator's role is to **facilitate** a dialogue that leads to a mutually beneficial agreement."*
    *   *(El rol del mediador es **facilitar** un diálogo que lleve a un acuerdo mutuamente beneficioso.)*

### **To mitigate**
1.  *"We must implement strict safety protocols to **mitigate** the risk of accidents in the laboratory."*
