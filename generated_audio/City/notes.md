Bienvenido a esta guía especializada en el vocabulario urbano y sociológico del inglés. Como profesor experto, he seleccionado estos términos basándome en los audios de la sección 'City'. ## 2. Ejemplos Prácticos Formales (Nivel B2/C1)

A continuación, se presentan dos oraciones formales y complejas para cada término clave. Observa el uso de conectores, pasivas y vocabulario académico.

### 🏗️ Arquitectura y Entorno Urbano
*   **Concrete jungle:** "Despite the aesthetic appeal of the skyline, many sociologists argue that living in a **concrete jungle** often leads to high stress levels due to the lack of natural light and greenery."
*   **Skyscrapers:** "The construction of new **skyscrapers** has transformed the city's identity, yet it raises concerns regarding structural integrity and energy consumption."
*   **Urban sprawl:** "Uncontrolled **urban sprawl** is a significant contributor to habitat loss and increased carbon emissions in metropolitan areas."
*   **Green spaces:** "City planners emphasize the importance of preserving **green spaces** to mitigate the urban heat island effect and improve public mental health."

### 🚶 Infraestructura y Movilidad
*   **Sidewalks / Pavements:** "Pedestrians must exercise caution when crossing busy streets, as **sidewalks** in older districts are often uneven and poorly maintained." (Nota: *Pavements* en UK).
*   **Public transport network:** "Investing in a robust **public transport network** is essential for reducing reliance on private vehicles and alleviating traffic congestion."
*   **Gridlock:** "The morning commute was severely delayed by gridlock caused by an accident on the main highway, affecting thousands of commuters."
*   **To commute to work:** "Many professionals prefer to **commute to work** via train rather than driving, citing the ability to read or relax during the journey."

### 💼 Estilo de Vida y Economía
*   **Hustle and bustle:** "The **hustle and bustle** of the financial district contrasts sharply with the quiet residential areas located in the suburbs."
*   **Skyrocketing rents:** "Due to **skyrocketing rents**, young graduates are increasingly forced to share accommodation or relocate to less expensive regions."
*   **Cost of living:** "The rising **cost of living** has prompted a debate among policymakers regarding affordable housing initiatives and wage adjustments."
*   **Job opportunities:** "Relocating to the capital city offers numerous **job opportunities**, though it comes with the trade-off of a significantly higher cost of living."

### 🌍 Sociedad y Medio Ambiente
*   **Air pollution:** "Residents in the valley often complain about poor air quality, which is exacerbated by industrial emissions and **air pollution** during winter months."
*   **Noise pollution:** "Urban planning regulations must address **noise pollution** from construction sites to ensure that residential areas remain suitable for sleep and rest."
*   **Inner-city slums:** "Government initiatives aim to rehabilitate **inner-city slums** by providing sanitation infrastructure and affordable housing solutions."
*   **Cosmopolitan atmosphere:** "The city's **cosmopolitan atmosphere** attracts international students and expatriates who appreciate the diversity of cuisines and cultural events."

### 🏠 Servicios y Vivienda
*   **Amenities:** "When choosing an apartment, it is crucial to consider the proximity to essential **amenities**, such as supermarkets, gyms, and schools."
*   **To be within walking distance:** "We are looking for a property that is **within walking distance** of our workplace to reduce our daily carbon footprint."
*   **Housing shortage:** "The current **housing shortage** has led to a speculative bubble in the real estate market, making it difficult for first-time buyers."

## 3. Collocations y Phrasal Verbs Asociados

Para dominar el nivel C1, es vital saber qué palabras suelen ir juntas (collocations) y cómo usar verbos frasales en este contexto.

| Palabra Clave | Collocations Comunes | Phrasal Verbs / Verbos + Preposiciones |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Buildings** | *High-rise buildings*, *Commercial buildings*, *Residential buildings* | *To build up* (construir), *To tear down* (demoler) |
| **Transport** | *Public transport network*, *Mass transit*, *Rapid transit* | *To commute to work*, *To get around* (moverse) |
| **Environment** | *Air pollution*, *Noise pollution*, *Green spaces*, *Eco-friendly* | *To clean up* (limpiar), *To pollute* (contaminar) |
| **Living** | *Cost of living*, *Quality of life*, *Way of life* | *To live on* (vivir de), *To live in* (vivir en) |
| **Rent** | *Skyrocketing rents*, *Affordable rent*, *Rental market* | *To pay off* (pagar), *To rent out* (alquilar) |
| **Stress** | *High stress levels*, *Manage stress*, *Cope with stress* | *To deal with*, *To put up with* (soportar) |
| **Traffic** | *Heavy traffic*, *Traffic congestion*, *Gridlock* | *To get stuck in* (quedarse atascado), *To cut through* (atravesar) |
| **City** | *Urban sprawl*, *Inner-city*, *Suburban city* | *To move into* (mudarse a), *To settle down* (establecerse) |

### 💡 Consejo del Profesor:
En el nivel C1, evita usar solo la palabra aislada. En lugar de decir "The rent is high", usa **"rents are skyrocketing"** o **"affordability crisis"**. Al hablar de ciudades, siempre especifica si te refieres al **UK** (*pavements*) o **US** (*sidewalks*), ya que esto demuestra un conocimiento cultural preciso.
