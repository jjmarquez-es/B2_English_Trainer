# 🏙️ Urban Vocabulary Study Guide (B2/C1 Level)

Welcome to your advanced English vocabulary session. Today, we are focusing on a set of high-frequency terms often encountered in contexts regarding **urban life, administration, and social issues**. While these words are not exclusively about the word "city," they are essential for describing city environments, governance, and community dynamics at an upper-intermediate level.

---

## 1. Reference Table: Vocabulary & Usage

| English Phrase | Spanish Translation | Phonetic / IPA | B2/C1 Usage Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shelter** | Refugio / Cobijo | `/ˈʃɛl.tər/` | Can be a noun (a place) or verb (to protect). In urban contexts, often refers to housing or emergency aid. |
| **Aim** | Objetivo / Pretender | `/eɪm/` | Noun: "Our aim is..." <br> Verb: "We aim to..." <br> *Note:* Often confused with "aim" (verb) vs "eye" (noun). |
| **Take over** | Asumir el control / Tomar posesión | `/teɪk ˈoʊ.vər/` | Phrasal verb. Means to assume responsibility or authority. Common in business and municipal contexts. |
| **Sympathetic** | Comprensivo / Solidario | `/sɪm.pəˈθɛt.ɪk/` | Adjective. Describes an attitude of understanding or support, not necessarily friendship. |
| **Amount** | Cantidad | `/əˈmaʊnt/` | Noun. Used with uncountable nouns (e.g., *an amount of money*). Do not use "amounts" for countable items unless pluralized specifically. |
| **Awareness** | Conciencia / Sensibilidad | `/əˈwɛr.nəs/` | Abstract noun. Often paired with "raise," "increase," or "social." Crucial for NGO and policy contexts. |
| **Crowded** | Concurrido / Llano | `/ˈkraʊ.dɪd/` | Adjective (Past Participle). Describes a place with too many people. *Note:* Do not confuse with "crowd" (noun). |
| **Confidence regained** | Confianza recuperada | `/kənˈfɪ.dəns rɪˈɡeɪnd/` | Noun Phrase. Refers to the restoration of trust, often in economic or political systems after a crisis. |

## 2. Formal Example Sentences (B2/C1)

Below are two formal sentences for each term, designed to demonstrate appropriate register and grammatical complexity suitable for C1 proficiency.

### **Shelter**
1. "The municipal council has allocated emergency funds to ensure that homeless individuals have access to adequate **shelter** during the winter months."
2. "While the city offers physical **shelter**, many residents still struggle with the psychological impact of housing insecurity."

### **Aim**
1. "Our primary **aim** is to integrate sustainable transport solutions into the urban planning framework by 2030."
2. "The mayor stated that her administration's **aim** was not merely to expand infrastructure, but to improve social equity within the district."

### **Take over**
1. "Following the merger, the new conglomerate will **take over** all operational responsibilities of the local transit authority next month."
2. "It is crucial for the interim manager to **take over** the project immediately to prevent delays in the construction timeline."

### **Sympathetic**
1. "The policy was designed to be **sympathetic** to small business owners who are struggling with rising rent prices in the downtown area."
2. "We need a **sympathetic** ear from the administration when discussing the challenges faced by long-term residents facing gentrification."

### **Amount**
1. "The **amount** of waste generated daily by the commercial district exceeds the current capacity of the recycling facilities."
2. "Despite the high **amount** of investment in green energy, the reduction in carbon emissions has been slower than anticipated."

### **Awareness**
1. "Public **awareness** regarding air quality standards has significantly increased since the implementation of the new industrial regulations."
2. "There is a growing need to raise **awareness** about mental health support services available for commuters and workers in high-stress environments."

### **Crowded**
1. "The subway lines are notoriously **crowded** during peak hours, which has led to calls for expanded rail capacity."
2. "Despite the **crowded** streets and heavy traffic, the city center remains a vibrant hub for cultural and economic activity."

### **Confidence regained**
1. "After the financial scandal was resolved, it took several years for investor **confidence regained** in the local stock market to stabilize."
*(Note: In natural speech, this is often phrased as "regained confidence," but as a study phrase, we treat it as a noun concept).*
2. "The transparency measures introduced by the new council helped ensure that public **confidence regained** was not misplaced in future governance decisions."

## 3. Phrasal Verbs & Collocations

To reach C1 proficiency, you must master how these words combine with other terms. Here are the most common collocations and phrasal verbs related to your list.

### **Take over**
*   **Take over responsibility:** *She decided to take over responsibility for the department.*
*   **Take over a company/project:** *The startup was acquired, and the new firm took over the project.*
*   **Take over from:** *He will take over from his predecessor as mayor next year.*

### **Awareness**
*   **Raise awareness:** *We need to raise awareness about water conservation.*
*   **Social awareness:** *The campaign focused on social awareness regarding homelessness.*
*   **Lack of awareness:** *Many citizens lack awareness of their voting rights.*

### **Crowded**
*   **Overcrowded:** *The city center is often overcrowded during the holiday season.*
*   **Crowded commute:** *She complained about her crowded commute to work.*
*   **Avoid crowded places:** *During the pandemic, people were advised to avoid crowded places.*

### **Sympathetic**
*   **Sympathetic ear:** *I need a sympathetic ear to talk about my problems.*
*   **Sympathetic response:** *The government received a sympathetic response from the public.*
*   **Be sympathetic to:** *The policy is sympathetic to the needs of low-income families.*

### **Shelter**
*   **Seek shelter:** *Residents were advised to seek shelter immediately.*
*   **Emergency shelter:** *The city opened an emergency shelter for the homeless.*
*   **Sheltered housing:** *There is a shortage of affordable sheltered housing in the suburbs.*

### **Amount**
*   **A significant amount:** *There was a significant amount of rain last week.*
*   **Large amount:** *The project required a large amount of funding.*
*   **Amount to:** *His actions amounted to negligence.* (Advanced usage)

### **Confidence regained**
*   **Regain confidence:** *The team worked hard to regain confidence after the loss.*
*   **Restore confidence:** *The bank needed to restore confidence in its customers.*
*   **Lose confidence:** *Investors lost confidence when the market crashed.*
